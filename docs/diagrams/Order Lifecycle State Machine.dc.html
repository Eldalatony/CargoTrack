STUCK VS. FAILED

DOCUMENTS_WITHHELD is a stuck state, not a failure. The shipment is delivered; release of documents is blocked on money. It loops forward to CLOSED_OUT the moment the final payment clears — no re-delivery, no new order.

FACTORY_CANNOT_FULFIL and QC_REJECTED are recoverable failures: each loops back one step or terminates at CANCELLED. Only CANCELLED and CLOSED_OUT are terminal.

TRANSITION CONTRACT

Every transition is executed server-side. Clients request a target status; they never write one. Each accepted transition writes one STATUS_HISTORY row and enqueues one notification job (see Notification Pipeline). Transitions are idempotent per (order_id, to_status, request_id).
